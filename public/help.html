GST Registration & TRN

What is a Temporary Reference Number (TRN) in Form GST REG-01?
A 15-digit Temporary Reference Number (TRN) is generated upon successful completion of Part-A of Form GST REG-01 after validating the applicant's PAN, Mobile Number, and Email ID via OTP. TRN is valid for 15 days, within which the applicant must complete and submit Part-B of the registration application.
What is the mandatory threshold limit for GST registration?
Under Section 22 of the CGST Act, registration is mandatory for businesses having an aggregate annual turnover exceeding ₹40 Lakhs for suppliers of goods (₹20 Lakhs for Special Category States) and ₹20 Lakhs for suppliers of services (₹10 Lakhs for Special Category States). Persons making inter-state taxable supplies must register mandatorily regardless of turnover.

Returns, GSTR-2B & Rule 88A ITC Offset

How does Rule 88A govern the order of Input Tax Credit (ITC) utilization?
Under Rule 88A, the ITC on account of Integrated Tax (IGST) must be completely exhausted first before utilizing CGST or SGST credits. The IGST credit is first offset against IGST output liability, and any remaining IGST credit can be utilized towards CGST and SGST liabilities in any order and in any proportion. CGST credit cannot be set off against SGST liability, and SGST credit cannot be set off against CGST liability.
What is Form GSTR-2B and when is it generated?
Form GSTR-2B is an auto-drafted Input Tax Credit (ITC) statement generated on the 14th of every month based on outward supply invoices filed by suppliers in their GSTR-1 returns up to the 11th/13th of that month. It classifies available eligible ITC and ineligible ITC as per Rule 36(4).

Tax Payments & PMT-06 Challans

What is a CPIN and CIN in GST payment?
A Common Portal Identification Number (CPIN) is a 14-digit unique number generated upon creating Form GST PMT-06 challan, valid for 15 days. When payment is successfully cleared by the authorized bank, the bank generates a 17-digit Challan Identification Number (CIN) containing a 3-character bank code plus a 14-digit number. Generation of CIN confirms crediting of the Electronic Cash Ledger.
What should I do if my bank account is debited but CIN is not generated?
If the amount is debited from your bank account but the CPIN status shows Pending, you can lodge an application in Form GST PMT-07 via the Grievance Redressal Portal or wait up to 2 hours for automated payment gateway reconciliation.

e-Way Bill (EWB-01)

When is generating an e-Way Bill compulsory?
Under Rule 138, generating an e-Way Bill is mandatory prior to the movement of goods in a vehicle where the consignment value (including tax) exceeds ₹50,000 for inter-state supplies, and for intra-state movement as notified by respective State GST rules.
